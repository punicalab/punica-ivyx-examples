# Returns and refunds

Kestrel Outdoor accepts returns of unused items within **45 days** of delivery.
Items bought in the end-of-season sale can be returned within **14 days** and
are refunded as store credit only.

## How a refund is paid

A refund goes back to the original payment method within 7 working days of the
item reaching the Bursa warehouse. A gift card purchase is refunded as a new
gift card.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Personalised items (engraved knives, printed jerseys).
- Opened gas canisters and fuel bottles.
- Socks and base layers once worn.
