# Warranty

Every Kestrel-branded tent, backpack and sleeping bag carries a **3-year**
warranty against manufacturing faults. Third-party brands carry the
manufacturer's own warranty, usually 2 years.

The warranty does not cover wear from normal use, UV damage, or repairs made by
anyone other than Kestrel's repair shop. A claim needs the order number and a
photo of the fault; the repair shop answers within 5 working days.
