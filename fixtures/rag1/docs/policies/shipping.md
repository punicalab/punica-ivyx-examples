# Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day leave the Bursa warehouse the same
day. Standard delivery inside Türkiye takes 2 to 4 working days and is free
above 1,500 TL; below that it costs 89 TL.

Express delivery (next working day) is available in Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir
for 179 TL. Islands and rural postcodes add one day to every estimate.

We do not ship gas canisters by air, so they are excluded from express orders.
